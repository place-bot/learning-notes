#psychometricsbasics

This part introduces the entry concepts of psychometrics, focusing on Classical Test Theory (CTT), Item Response Theory (IRT), and why modern measurement requires model-based explanations.

## Learning sequence

1. [Overview of CTT and IRT](../ctt_irt_intro.md): First establish the basic differences between the two sets of measurement theories.
2. [Model-based measurement](../modelbased.md): Understand again why item reactions, latent traits and model assumptions should be discussed together.

## What questions does this part answer?

- What are observed score, true score and measurement error.
- Why the same total score does not necessarily have the same meaning under different item combinations.
- Why does IRT include item parameters (item parameters) and participant ability (ability, \(\theta\)) into the model at the same time.
- Why the later IRT, CDM and scoring chapters rely on this concept.
