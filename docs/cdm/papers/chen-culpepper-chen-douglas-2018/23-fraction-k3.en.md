## Content explanation

According to the item that only loads one attribute, the author interprets the three columns as:

1. Find the common denominator;
2. Borrow from the integer part;
3. Subtract the integer and fractional parts separately.

This explanation helps explain:

- \(3\frac12-2\frac32\) and \(4\frac4{12}-2\frac7{12}\) require only the second attribute in restricted methods;
- \(3-2\frac15\) requires both first and third attributes in a restricted method.

## item parameters difference

The Q difference will change the ideal response grouping and subsequently change \(s,g\). The original article specifically points out the slipping differences between the three questions:
