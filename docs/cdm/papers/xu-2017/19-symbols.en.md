# symbol table

## Base object

|symbol|Dimension or value|meaning|
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(N\) |positive integer|number of participants|
| \(J\) |positive integer|number of items|
| \(K\) |positive integer|Number of binary attributes|
| \(i\) | \(1,\ldots,N\) |participant index|
| \(j\) | \(1,\ldots,J\) |item index|
| \(k\) | \(1,\ldots,K\) |Property index|
| \(R_j\) | \(\{0,1\}\) |Response to question \(j\)|
| \(\boldsymbol R\) | \(\{0,1\}^J\) |complete response vector|
| \(\boldsymbol r\) | \(\{0,1\}^J\) |A reactive pattern or item subset indicator|
| \(\boldsymbol\alpha\) | \(\{0,1\}^K\) |attribute profile|
| \(\boldsymbol0,\boldsymbol1\) |bipartite vector|All zeros and all ones vector|
| \(\boldsymbol e_k\) |unit vector|Pattern containing only attribute \(k\)|

## Q and partial ordering

|symbol|meaning|
| --- | --- |
| \(Q\) |\(J\times K\) item—attribute matrix|
| \(q_{jk}\) |Does question \(j\) require the attribute \(k\)?|
| \(\boldsymbol q_j\) |Line \(j\) of Q|
| \(\boldsymbol\alpha\succeq\boldsymbol q_j\) |\(\boldsymbol\alpha\) has all the required attributes for this question|
| \(\boldsymbol\alpha\nsucceq\boldsymbol q_j\) |At least one required attribute is missing|
| \(I_K\) |\(K\times K\) identity matrix|
| \(Q'\) |The remaining rows after the two unit blocks in C1|

## Proof tool

|symbol|meaning|
| --- | --- |
| \(\boldsymbol\theta^*\) |One translation constant for each question|
| \(\boldsymbol\theta^*\boldsymbol1^\top\) |A matrix that repeats the corresponding translation constants for each row|
| \(D(\boldsymbol\theta^*)\) |Reversible lower triangular row transformation of Proposition 3|
| \(\boldsymbol u_k\) |Zero class/single attribute class comparison row vector generated by C2|
| \(b_k\) |\(\boldsymbol u_k\) leaves a non-zero value on the \(\boldsymbol e_k\) vector|
| \(\boldsymbol m\) |Column selector constructed from full rank \(T\)-submatrix|

## Conditions and conclusions

|Name|Mathematical content|
| --- | --- |
| complete Q |Q contains one \(I_K\)|
| C1 |Q contains two non-overlapping \(I_K\)|
| C2 |The probability vectors of each \(\boldsymbol e_k\) and \(\boldsymbol0\) on \(Q'\) are different|
| strict identifiability |The same observation distribution implies that the parameters are the same item by item.|

## Quick Dimension Check

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{object}&\text{Dimension}\\\hline
Q&J\times K\\
\Theta&J\times2^K\\
\boldsymbol p&2^K\times1\\
T(Q,\Theta)&2^J\times2^K\\
D(\boldsymbol\theta^*)&2^J\times2^J
\end{array}
\]

\(D\) is large in theory, but the proof requires only its algebraic existence. Implementations can directly construct rows from the desired subset of items without having to explicitly save the entire \(2^J\times2^J\) matrix for large tests.
