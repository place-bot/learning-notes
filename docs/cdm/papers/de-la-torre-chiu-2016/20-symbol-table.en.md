## Three easily confused asterisks and superscripts

\[
K_j^*
\]

Indicates the number of required attributes of the item under its real or current definition.

\[
\boldsymbol q^*
\]

Represents a correct q-vector.

\[
\widehat{\varsigma}_{\max}^2
\]

Indicates the maximum GDI of samples generated by all-attribute candidates.

## Four probability levels

|Probability|Conditions|Purpose|
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(P(Y_j=1\mid\boldsymbol\alpha)\) |full mode|original reaction function|
| \(P(Y_j=1\mid\boldsymbol\alpha_{\boldsymbol q})\) |Candidate reduction pattern|Success rate after folding|
| \(P(\boldsymbol\alpha_i=\boldsymbol\alpha\mid\boldsymbol Y_i)\) |All student responses|posterior soft classification|
| \(P(\alpha_{ik}=1\mid\theta_i)\) |higher level abilities|Simulate attribute generation|
