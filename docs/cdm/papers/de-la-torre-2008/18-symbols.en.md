## Three easily confused quantities

\[
g_j=P(X_j=1\mid\eta_j=0),
\]

\[
s_j=P(X_j=0\mid\eta_j=1),
\]

\[
\delta_j=(1-s_j)-g_j.
\]

So:

- The correct answer rate of the mastery group is \(1-s_j\);
- The correct answer rate of the nonmastery group is \(g_j\);
- The difference between the two groups is \(1-s_j-g_j\).
