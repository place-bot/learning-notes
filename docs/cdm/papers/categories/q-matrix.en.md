# Q Matrix Verification and Learning: 14 articles

1. [de la Torre (2008): Empirical Q-matrix validation of DINA](../de-la-torre-2008/index.md). 21 pages completed, covering \(\delta\) indicator, sequential EM search, all simulations and real data, code implementation and runnable reproduction.
2. [de la Torre & Chiu (2016): General Empirical Q-Matrix Validation](../de-la-torre-chiu-2016/index.md). 23 pages completed, covering GDI/PVAF, all proofs, two sets of simulations, fractional subtraction data, consistency arguments, `GDINA` source code, and runnable reproductions.
3. [Liu, Xu & Ying (2012): Data-driven Q matrix learning](../liu-xu-ying-2012/index.md). Completed 24 pages covering \(T\)-matrix, three objective functions, DINA EM, line-by-line search, four sets of simulations, numerical errata, code boundaries, and runnable reproduction.
4. [Liu, Xu & Ying (2013): Self-learning Q matrix theory](../liu-xu-ying-2013/index.md). 30 pages have been completed, covering C1--C5, three consistency theorems, all propositions and appendix proofs, evidence boundaries, code implementation and computable verification.
5. [Chen et al. (2018): Bayesian DINA Q matrix estimation](../chen-culpepper-chen-douglas-2018/index.md). 30 pages completed, covering Q-identifiable priors, DS1/DS2, irreducibility and symmetry, MH/restricted Gibbs, all simulation and empirical results, original supplementary code, `edina` package, and computable verification.
6. [Gu & Xu (2021): Necessary and sufficient conditions for joint identifiability of Q matrices](../gu-xu-2021/index.md). 34 pages completed, covering strict and general recognition, A/B/C of DINA, D/E of general RLCM, all proof routes, seven sets of simulations, official MATLAB code and independent candidate space verification.
7. [Zhao & Huang (2019): Automatic recognition of Q matrix](../zhao-huang-2019/index.md) for text classification. 30 pages have been completed, covering 1,069 question nine attribute data, 805 question two classification clipping, three-stage NLP pipeline, Tables 1--3, majority class and small test set audit, F1 formula bias, independent implementation and CAT interface.
8. Qin & Guo (2024): machine learning improves Q matrix verification.
9. Fan, Bialo & Li (2026): AI tool development and verification Q matrix.
10. Li, Ma & Xu (2022): RBM learning for large-scale Q matrices.
11. Oka & Okada (2023): Scalable Bayesian Q estimation for stochastic optimization and variational inference.
12. Liu, Andersson & Skrondal (2020): Constrained MHRM Q-matrix estimation.
13. Chen et al. (2021): Attribute quantity inference for exploratory DINA.
14. Yamaguchi (2025): Bayesian DCM with partially known Q matrices.

Each article will distinguish between Q validation, Q estimation, Q identification and attribute number selection. For complete metadata, see [General Index of Papers](../../paper-index.md).
