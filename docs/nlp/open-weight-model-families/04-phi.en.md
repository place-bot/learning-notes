# Phi: Improve the capability density of small models with data quality

## 1. Core research questions

The Phi series revolves around an ongoing question: Can smaller models achieve high unit parameter capabilities on inference, coding, and general language tasks if the training data is rigorously filtered, synthesized, and organized curriculum-style?

Common scale routes can be summarized as:

```text
Phi-1 / Phi-1.5
  Emphasis on textbook-quality and synthetic data
        ↓
Phi-2
  Expanded data blending and general capabilities
        ↓
Phi-3 / Phi-3.5
  Client-side friendly, long context, multi-language and multi-modal branches
        ↓
Phi-4 family
  14B basic capabilities, reasoning models, mini and multi-modal versions
```

## 2. What does “Textbook-quality data” mean?

There is a huge amount of Internet corpus, but it contains repetitive, incomplete, noisy and lack of teaching structure. Phi's research path increases the ratio of:

- A text with clear logic and gradually developed concepts;
- Filtered high-quality web pages and code;
- Synthetic examples generated by stronger models and filtered by rules or models;
- Task data designed for reasoning, math and coding abilities.

Data selection can be written as weighted empirical risk:

\[
\mathcal L(\theta)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}w(x_i)\,
\ell\bigl(f_\theta(x_i),y_i\bigr),
\]

where \(w(x_i)\) represents the quality of the sample, goal relevance, or course stage weight. Improving data quality is not simply about deleting low-quality text. It also includes deciding which concepts the model should learn first and what difficulty distribution it should practice repeatedly.

## 3. Why synthetic data may be effective

Synthetic data enables precise control of:

- Coverage of question types and knowledge points;
- length of reasoning steps;
- Ratio of positive and negative examples;
- Language and output format;
- Density of difficult samples.

But synthetic data will also inherit the bias and errors of the teacher model. If a large number of training sets come from the same generator, the student model may learn teacher-specific expression templates and even cause contamination near the evaluation set. A reliable pipeline requires generation, filtering, deduplication, authenticity checking and manual spot checks, rather than just expanding the synthetic sample size.

## 4. Phi-3 and small deployments

Phi-3-mini is a model of approximately 3.8B parameters, officially available in 4K and 128K context versions, and released with ONNX, quantization, and cross-platform deployment options. The practical advantages of small models come from a combination of reduced costs:

\[
\text{memory for weights}
\approx
P\times b/8,
\]

Among them, \(P\) is the parameter quantity, and \(b\) is the number of digits for each parameter. When the 3.8B model uses 4-bit weight quantization, the theoretical lower limit of only the weight is about 1.9 GB; KV cache, activation, quantization metadata and framework overhead must also be added during runtime.

Therefore, the small number of parameters creates conditions for end-side operation, but the above theoretical values cannot be used to judge whether the equipment is sufficient.

## 5. Phi-4 data route

Phi-4 is a 14B dense decoder-only model. Official model cards and technical reports put data quality at the center of the training recipe: pretraining mixes synthetic data, filtered public web pages and code, and adds supervised fine-tuning and direct preference optimization after pretraining. The official model card reports a training size of approximately 9.8T tokens and 16K context.

An important change is revealed here: the small model route is not the same as the small data route. When the model parameters are small, large-scale, repeated screening and highly structured data training can still be used to compress more capabilities into limited parameters.

## 6. MIT License and Open Scope

Many official Microsoft licenses, such as Phi-3-mini and Phi-4, use the MIT license, which has relatively few restrictions on commercial use and modification. Still need to check separately:

- Check whether the precise checkpoint is officially released by Microsoft;
- Whether the model card lists additional usage restrictions;
- Whether the inference code, quantitative transformations and third-party data used are otherwise licensed;
- Whether high-risk areas meet application layer regulations and testing requirements.

## 7. Capability Boundary

Phi is suitable for resource constraints, low latency, offline processing, distillation teachers or narrow domain fine-tuning. Small models will still lag behind larger models in long-tail knowledge, complex tool planning, cross-language stability, and difficult open reasoning. When choosing Phi, you should pay attention to the measured pass rate of the target task, rather than directly converting "strong unit parameter capability" into "sufficient for all tasks".
