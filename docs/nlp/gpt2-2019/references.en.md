## Interpretation and Review Agreement

- Paper numbers are recorded according to the 2019 official report form;
- The model parameter count is also marked with subsequent revisions in the official repository;
- The code chapters are based on `model.py`, `encoder.py` and `sample.py` from the official `openai/gpt-2` warehouse;
- The reclassification of "zero-shot" is based on the GPT-3 paper's definition of zero/one/few-shot.
