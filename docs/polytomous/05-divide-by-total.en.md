# 5. Concept Supplement 2: Divide-by-Total Model

## 5.1 Definition

Category-total modeling refers to a modeling approach in which the score for a category, dimension, or subgroup is divided by the total score or total to reflect its relative proportion within the whole. This structure is commonly used in scenarios such as multidimensional scoring, cognitive diagnosis, and behavior proportion analysis.

The basic idea is:

\[
\text{Proportion} = \frac{\text{Score or quantity in a category}}{\text{overall score or quantity}}
\]

## 5.2 Application examples

**Subdimension scale score**

In personality or psychological scales, sometimes researchers want to know the proportion of a certain dimension (such as "anxiety") in an individual's overall response:

\[
\text{Anxiety proportion} = \frac{\text{Anxiety dimension total score}}{\text{scaletotal score}}
\]

This method can be used to compare the relative feature distribution of different participants in each dimension.

**Response Proportional Modeling**

In response time or response pattern analyses, researchers sometimes count the proportion of a certain type of response among the total responses, for example:

\[
P_i = \frac{\text{Number of quick responses}}{\text{Total number of reactions}}
\]

This type of proportional indicators can be used as characteristic input of individual behavioral styles for modeling tasks such as clustering and prediction.

**Proportion of Mastery in Cognitive Diagnosis**

In the cognitive diagnosis model (CDM), researchers sometimes calculate the proportion of the number of skills mastered by a participant to the total number of skills to measure the overall mastery level:

\[
\text{Master the proportions} = \frac{\text{Number of skills mastered by participant}}{\text{Total number of skills}}
\]

This form is commonly seen in posterior analysis, capability classification, or policy modeling.

## 5.3 Comparison with traditional models

|Model type|Modeling form|Features|
| --- | --- | --- |
|Classification-total model|Relative scale modeling|Emphasize the proportional structure of part/whole|
|Classic IRT model (such as 2PL)|Probabilistic modeling based on latent variables|Model the match between individuals and items|
|CDM model|Modeling knowledge status and attribute mastery|Suitable for diagnostic and classification measurements|

## 5.4 Summary

Classification-total model is not a standard name for IRT model, but generally refers to a type of structure modeled by "local score divided by total score". Its core is to extract **structural proportion information** to reflect the relative characteristics of individuals in different dimensions, categories or behavior types.

If the term is encountered in specific literature, modeling frameworks, or software outputs, its definition and modeling purpose should be further clarified in context.
