# 15. Undescribed model

Due to space limitations, several potentially important models are not described in this chapter:

**1. Multiple Choice Model:** Thissen & Steinberg (1984)

**2. Continuous interval model:** Rost (1988)

**3. Ordered Partition Model:** Wilson (1992)

**4. Multi-dimensional expansion:**

- Log-linear multidimensional IRT parameterization of Kelderman and Rijkes (1994)
- Muraki and Carlson (1995) full information item-factor analysis method

**5. Other multidimensional models:** Several multidimensional models described in Chapter 4 have multiple generalizations
