# 16. How many subjects are needed?

## 16.1 FAQ

Students and colleagues often ask: How many subjects are needed to estimate IRT model parameters?

### 16.1.1 There is no standard answer

**Reason:**

- Depends on many factors
- How different is the item?
- How many item parameters to estimate

### 16.1.2 Monte Carlo simulation study

**Study by Reise and Yu (1990):**

- Shows that GRM can be estimated with as few as 250 subjects using MULTILOG
- But they recommend about 500 people

Limitations of simulation study

- Only useful if data matches simulated data
- Actual data may differ from simulated conditions

## 16.2 Alternative approach suggestions

**Based on standard error criteria:**
It is recommended that researchers have enough subjects to make the standard error of parameter estimation reasonably small.

**Definition of "reasonably small":**

- Depends on the researcher’s measurement goals
- The impact of item parameters estimation on trait level estimation is a final issue that many researchers are concerned about

## 16.3 Implications of the data in this chapter

As can be seen from the results of this chapter, even with 350 subjects, some category threshold parameter estimates in GRM are not ideal.
